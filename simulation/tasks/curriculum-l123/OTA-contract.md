# OTA measurement contract
TT, 27 C, 1.8 V supply and 100 pF load. Interface: `.subckt OTA VDD GND VINP VINN VOUT IBIAS`.
Pass ibias_uA separately: 1..40 uA in 0.1 uA steps. The external bias current flows from IBIAS to GND; provide a DC path from VDD to IBIAS.
Use ordinary SKY130 1.8 V MOS X instances, W=0.42..10 um and L=0.15..5 um in 0.01 um steps, integer M=1..500. W/L are bare micrometre values. Resistors: 0.1..1000 kOhm in 0.01 kOhm steps, using k suffix. Capacitors: 1..100 pF in 0.01 pF steps, using p suffix. Do not include sources, models, analysis commands or measurement fixtures. Topology may change.
Use `arena contract --profile sky130-ota` for device and measurement details. Input common mode is 0.45 V. GBW is the first valid downward 0 dB crossing; phase margin uses signed unwrapped phase. Rejection ratios are measured at 0.1 Hz. Gain and rejection use dB, GBW MHz, slew rate V/us and power mW. Area score is sqrt(sum(W*L*M)+1089*sum(C_pF)+5*sum(R_kOhm)), excluding the external fixture.
The transient follower input steps from 0.3 to 0.5 V after 2 us, with 1 ns edges and 4 us high time, ending at 10 us. Both directions require valid slew and settling measurements; stable levels must be within 1% of their targets. Functional checks also inspect rails, overshoot and crossing direction. Numerical constraints are defined in tasks.json; the evaluator implementation defines exact windows and diagnostics.
