# SRAM measurement contract
TT, 27 C, 1.8 V. Interface: `.subckt DUT Q QB BL BLB WL VDD VSS`.
Use a standard 6T cell: two cross-coupled CMOS inverters and two WL-controlled NMOS access devices. Ordinary SKY130 1.8 V MOS X instances only, W=0.42..10 um, L=0.15..1 um, 0.01 um grid, M=1. Bodies connect to their corresponding supplies. Names, source/drain orientation and left/right sizing may vary. No sources, initial conditions, models or measurement commands inside the DUT.
The fixture adds 50 fF per bitline and 1 fF per storage node, using Gear integration with 2 ps maximum step. Initialize by a real write, then repeat write0/read0/write1/read1 twice. Each operation lasts 168 ns, including 100 ns hold. Writes must flip the previous state; reads must not disturb it.
Write delay measures WL crossing 0.9 V to correct storage levels (at most 0.3 V / at least 1.5 V), maintained through the check window. Read delay measures WL crossing 0.9 V to a correctly directed 50 mV bitline differential maintained until WL falls. Worst values over four writes and four reads are reported.
Read SNM is the smaller largest axis-aligned square in the two butterfly lobes under WL=BL=BLB=1.8 V. It requires bistability and agreement within 0.5 mV between 1 mV and 0.1 mV sweeps. Energy integrates positive cell, write, precharge and wordline supply power, taking the worst operation in fJ. Standby uses the worst final 10 ns write-hold supply power in nW. Area is sum(W*L), not layout area. All dynamic checks, DC bistability checks and six task metrics must pass. See tasks.json and the evaluator for exact timing and thresholds.
